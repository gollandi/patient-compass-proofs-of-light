Reliable sources.
Auditable clinical review.
Accountability for every single word.

The Helm is where clinicians prepare, review and approve the information behind the patient experience. AI can help prepare a draft. Clinical approval remains human.

Polaris supports preparation before the procedure, with standard and Easy Read options.

  • No patient log-in. Patients open a link. The Suite holds no patient accounts and no directly identifying patient information.
  • Standard and Easy Read, together. Every step is prepared in both forms. A figure present in one and missing from the other blocks sign-off.
  • The link stays current. A patient’s link opens the published pathway as it stands today, not a saved copy.
  • Figures carry their source. A risk figure names where it comes from, and can be shown as people out of 100.

About the evidence

The homecoming image is AI-generated and depicts no real patient. The paper doorway is an abstract illustration. Its printed title, ‘Discharge Summary’, belongs to the artwork and does not describe a feature of the Suite. It represents continued access to information, not clinical monitoring, an emergency service or a clinician available on demand.

The Compass card is actual demonstration content from the existing product library; its capture date is not established. It does not represent an individual patient record. Clinical approval and a published edition are distinct from evidence that someone received, opened or read information.

The pile of leaflets is an AI-generated illustration; it does not depict any organisation’s actual materials. Polaris supports preparation and the consent conversation. It does not replace an individual discussion with the treating clinician, and it does not establish that a patient has understood or consented.

The page held to the light is an AI-generated illustration. The printed heading, the Suite mark and the three imprints are drawn over it to describe the clinical process; they do not depict a real document.

No reduction in calls, time saving or clinical outcome is claimed. Trial wording was checked against the public pilot page on 24 September 2026. This is an introduction, not clinical advice.

On patient-compass.co.uk this page reports aggregate, cookieless usage to Plausible (which scene was chosen, which link was followed), under the privacy policy. It sets no cookies and identifies no one.
